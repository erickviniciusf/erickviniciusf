## 🚀 About me

I spent 7 years in luxury hospitality before writing my first line of code. That taught me something no course does: **the problem a client reports is almost never the real problem.** That is exactly what I do now in software engineering.

I am a Full Stack Developer pursuing a degree in **Systems Analysis and Development** in Brasília. I build real products — with documented architecture decisions, automated tests, and the boring parts that separate a portfolio project from a system that survives production.

What that means in practice, across the two products I am building:

- 🤖 **Stay.o** — AI-powered SaaS for the hospitality industry, built from scratch. Backend 100% complete: conversational AI over the WhatsApp Business API, JWT authentication, 8 relational MySQL tables, scheduled jobs and an operational alerting system.
- 📱 **Offline-first PWA** — TypeScript monorepo with Fastify and React 19. Works without internet because the user needs it exactly when there is no signal. Idempotent payment webhooks, an AI layer with guardrails enforced by architecture (not by prompt), LGPD compliance, and every decision recorded as an ADR.

I do not list "familiar with X" in my stack. If it is there, I shipped something with it.

### PWA offline-first — *em construção*

> Aplicação que precisa funcionar exatamente quando o usuário não tem sinal.
> Repositório privado durante o desenvolvimento.

**Decisões que definem o projeto:**
- 📦 Monorepo **TypeScript** com `pnpm workspaces` — API, web e contratos compartilhados
- ⚡ **Fastify 5** + **Prisma 7** sobre MySQL, com validação fim a fim em **Zod**
- 📱 **React 19** + **Vite** + **Tailwind 4**, PWA com precache e rotas protegidas offline
- 🔐 Sessão com **Argon2** e **JWT (jose)**, com concessão de acesso válida sem rede
- 💳 Webhooks de pagamento **idempotentes** — responde antes de processar, descarta reenvio
- 🧠 Camada de IA com **guardrails garantidos por arquitetura**, não por prompt
- 📋 **LGPD** por design, diagramas **C4** e cada decisão registrada em **ADR**
- ✅ **Vitest**, CI no **GitHub Actions**, staging via **Docker Compose**

`TypeScript` `Fastify` `Prisma` `React 19` `Vite` `Tailwind` `Zod` `Vitest` `Docker`
